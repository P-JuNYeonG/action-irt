## Item Classification

Items are classified into three types based on the functional distribution of selected actions:

| Type | Label | Items |
|------|-------|-------|
| 1 | **Outcome-dominant** — Most selected actions are final-choice operations | PS1-3, PS1-4, PS1-5, PS1-7, PS2-3 |
| 2 | **Process-dominant** — Selected actions span intermediate process steps | PS1-1, PS1-2, PS2-1, PS2-5, PS2-6 |
| 3 | **Hybrid** — Outcome-proximal and process actions coexist | PS1-6, PS2-2, PS2-4, PS2-7 |

## Case Studies

Three representative items are presented in the main text:

### CD Tally (PS1-3) — Outcome-dominant

Three actions selected. The correct-answer combobox selection (`combobox_menulist_index9`) dominates with ΔE = 0.273. Behavioral discrimination is concentrated at the final choice.

### Party Invitations Part 1 (PS1-1) — Process-dominant

Sixteen actions selected. Core mail-sorting operations carry the largest positive effects. Several actions show frequency–sign discordance, where actions more common among correct respondents carry negative residual effects after ability adjustment.

### Lamp Return (PS2-7) — Hybrid

Eight actions selected. A systematic anterior–posterior sign pattern: early procedural steps carry negative ΔE despite being performed mainly by correct respondents, while later completion steps carry positive ΔE.
